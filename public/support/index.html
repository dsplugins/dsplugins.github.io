Scope Planner is built and supported by DSPlugins. Support is by email, and every message is answered by the people who build the app.

How to reach us#

Write to support@dsplugins.dev — questions, bug reports and feature requests all go to the same address.

Many answers are already in the user guide: setup, every feature, and a troubleshooting table.

Service level#

  • Channel: email, support@dsplugins.dev.
  • Support hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00 Kyiv time (UTC+2; UTC+3 in summer), except public holidays in Ukraine.
  • First reply: we aim to reply within one business day.

The first-reply time is a target, not a guarantee. As the terms of use say, support is provided on a reasonable-effort basis and no response time or service level is guaranteed. A message that arrives outside support hours counts from the start of the next business day.

To get an answer in one round#

Include:

  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps most;
  • which screen you were on (estimation table, Sprint Preview, 🗂 Drafts, admin page);
  • for a failed publish, the reason text from the result banner;
  • for a licensing question, your Jira site address (your-site.atlassian.net).

Please do not send issue content you would not want to share outside your organisation — a description of the problem is almost always enough.

Security issues#

To report a vulnerability, write to the same address with Security in the subject line. Security reports are handled ahead of other requests, and confirmed vulnerabilities are fixed as a priority. How the app handles data is described in the privacy policy.
