Scope Planner

Estimate a whole sprint on one screen — then publish the numbers into Jira.

Scope Planner is a Jira Cloud app for teams that estimate in batches instead of one issue at a time. Pick a scope, fill in the estimates in a single table, keep it as a private draft while you think, share it with the team when you are ready, and publish it to Jira’s own estimate fields when the numbers are agreed.

It runs entirely on Atlassian’s infrastructure, built on Atlassian Forge. There are no external servers, no data egress, and no tracking of any kind — the app declares no external network permissions, so the Forge platform itself blocks outbound calls. The only personal data it stores is Atlassian account IDs.

How it works

  1. Choose a scope — a board and sprint, a project, or your own JQL.
  2. Estimate in one table — story points on a numeric or T-shirt scale, or time estimates. Inline edits, bulk fill, and running totals as you go.
  3. Keep a draft — private while you work; share it with the team when it is ready. Drafts do not touch Jira, so nothing is written until you decide.
  4. Review and publish — Sprint Preview shows exactly what will change before anything is written, and every write executes with your own Jira permissions.
