DSPlugins builds focused apps for Jira Cloud that do one job well and keep your data where it already lives. Our apps run entirely on Atlassian Forge: no external servers, no data egress, no tracking.

What we make#

Scope Planner is our sprint estimation app. It puts a whole sprint on one screen, keeps work in private and team drafts, and publishes to Jira only when you decide. The user guide covers every feature.

You can find our apps on our Atlassian Marketplace partner page.

Why the articles#

We write about estimating and planning in Jira because we spend our days on it. The articles start from what Jira can do without any app, link to Atlassian’s own documentation where it applies, and say plainly when an app of ours is the subject.

Contact#

Every message is answered by the people who build the apps. Write to support@dsplugins.dev, or see the support page for hours and what to include.
