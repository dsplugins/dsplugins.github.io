How it works

From scope to Jira in four steps

Nothing touches your Jira issues until the last one — and you see exactly what it will write before you take it.

  1. 1

    Choose a scope

    A board’s sprint or backlog, a project, or any JQL you write.

  2. 2

    Estimate in one table

    Story points on a numeric or T-shirt scale, original and remaining time. Inline edits, bulk fill and running totals as you go.

  3. 3

    Draft, then share

    Your work auto-saves to a private draft. Share it and it becomes the team draft; teammates merge their numbers in through a row-by-row review.

  4. 4

    Review, then publish

    Sprint Preview puts your value next to Jira’s, issue by issue. Publishing Pro writes them in one pass, with your own Jira permissions.

Estimate

The whole sprint on one screen

Stop opening issues one at a time. Load a sprint, a backlog or any JQL into a spreadsheet-style table and estimate every issue right where you see it.

  • Story points, original and remaining estimate — inline, or one value applied to a whole selection at once.
  • Five scales: Fibonacci, Modified Fibonacci, Powers of 2, Linear and T-shirt sizes. Values snap to the scale, so nobody types 7.
  • Totals update as you type and break down by epic, assignee, label or type.

Review

Every change, before Jira sees it

Estimates stay in a draft until you decide. Sprint Preview lists exactly what will change, issue by issue: your value next to what Jira holds now.

  • Every edit lands in a private draft only you can see.
  • Share it and it becomes the team draft. Teammates merge their numbers in through a row-by-row review, so nobody overwrites anyone by accident.
  • Untick anything you want to leave alone, then publish Pro — with your own Jira permissions.
  • If an issue fails, the rest still land, the reason is shown, and Retry repeats only the failures.

Privacy by architecture

Your data never leaves Atlassian

Scope Planner runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. Its manifest declares no external network permissions, so the platform itself blocks any outbound call — enforced by Atlassian, not merely promised by us.

No servers of ours

Drafts and preferences live in Forge hosted storage, inside your own Atlassian site. We receive no copies of your data.

Your permissions, always

Every Jira read and write runs as you, so the app can’t see or change anything you couldn’t in Jira directly.

Account IDs only

People are identified by Atlassian account ID; names are fetched live, never stored. Private data is erased automatically when an account is deleted.

No tracking — here either

No analytics, cookies or advertising in the app. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from anywhere else.

Free and Pro

Estimate for free. Publish with Pro.

Everyone on an installed site can estimate, keep drafts and preview the change. Pro writes the agreed numbers into Jira and adds the planning views.

Free

Included with the app

  • The estimation table — inline and bulk editing, numeric and T-shirt scales, time estimates
  • Private drafts and team-shared drafts
  • Totals, breakdown, and column visibility
  • Sprint Preview — see what a publish would change

Pro

Subscription through the Atlassian Marketplace

Everything in Free, plus:

  • Publishing estimates to Jira (and retrying a failed publish)
  • CSV export
  • Per-assignee workload with capacity warnings
  • Estimation history per issue
  • Auto-calculated target velocity from closed sprints

Pro features are unlocked by a subscription or an active evaluation obtained through the Atlassian Marketplace, which handles all billing. Without one you can still estimate and keep drafts — you just cannot write the estimates into Jira. Your drafts are never deleted by a licence change.

Questions

Asked before installing

Not here? The user guide covers every feature, or write to us.

Does anything reach Jira while we estimate?

No. Drafts live in the app’s own storage, not in your issues. Nothing is written to Jira until someone reviews the change in Sprint Preview and publishes it.

Can a teammate overwrite my numbers?

Not by accident. Only your private draft is edited directly; changes reach the team draft whole, through a review screen that shows row by row what will change.

What works without a Pro subscription?

The estimation table, private and team drafts, totals and Sprint Preview. You cannot publish estimates into Jira — your drafts are kept, and publishing becomes available as soon as a subscription or trial is active.

What does a Jira admin need to set up?

Once per site: which Jira fields hold your estimates. 🔍 Detect Fields scans your fields and suggests the mappings; the user guide walks through it.

Where is our data stored?

In Atlassian Forge hosted storage, inside your own Atlassian site. The developer operates no infrastructure of its own and receives no copies of your data. Details are in the privacy policy.
