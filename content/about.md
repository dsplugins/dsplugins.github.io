# About DSPlugins

DSPlugins builds focused apps for Jira Cloud that do one job well and keep your data where it already
lives. Our apps run entirely on Atlassian Forge: no external servers, no data egress, no tracking.

## What we make

**[Scope Planner](/)** is our sprint estimation app. It puts a whole sprint on one screen, keeps work
in private and team drafts, and publishes to Jira only when you decide. The [user guide](/guide/)
covers every feature.

You can find our apps on our [Atlassian Marketplace partner
page](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/vendors/1218450/).

## Why the articles

We write about estimating and planning in Jira because we spend our days on it. [The
articles](/articles/) start from what Jira can do without any app, link to Atlassian's own
documentation where it applies, and say plainly when an app of ours is the subject.
